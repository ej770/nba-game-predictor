Behind the picks

Pre-game winner

Repository model picks use Elo, rest, recent point differential and home-court trends. The published walk-forward test correctly picked 66.4% of winners across 4,920 regular-season games from 2022–23 through 2025–26.

Score projection

The model total adds both projected scores. The fair spread gives the projected favorite a negative handicap. A pregame ESPN total, when available, supplies the comparison for Over or Under. The daily score feed carries the previous season’s results into opening night. The score estimate averages each team’s last ten regular-season scoring performances with its opponent’s points allowed. Home court adds 1.5 points to the home score and subtracts 1.5 from the away score.

When a repository pick is unavailable, the board labels the win estimate “Score estimate.” It uses the projected margin and an assumed 12-point spread in outcomes. Its accuracy has not been backtested.
